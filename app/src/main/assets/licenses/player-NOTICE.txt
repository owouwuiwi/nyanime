Nyanime native player

The native MPV/FFmpeg closure is rebuilt from the immutable revisions in
tools/native/player/sources.lock.json, with GPL/version3 enabled and nonfree
disabled. FFmpegKit callbacks, SAF support, the Android JNI bridge, subtitles,
user shaders and the generic HLS seek patches are retained.

Original upstream notices and full license documents are included in
licenses/native-player and licenses/native. The original Java/Kotlin APIs are
retained from the pinned upstream AARs; their source snapshots are included in
the supplemental source archive. GPL applies to distribution of the combined
player; the MIT/LGPL licenses of the bridge sources remain intact.

The release page provides the application and native source archives,
configuration records, patches, dependency snapshots and checksums:
https://github.com/owouwuiwi/nyanime/releases

See tools/native/README.md and docs/licensing.md for rebuilding and attribution.
Production signing credentials are not needed to build a modified application.
