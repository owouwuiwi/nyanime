# Two-page reading

The Reading mode selector has a sixth, independent on/off option: Spread. Enable it to pair original pages without changing reading direction. Reader settings → Reading also offers Spread, continuous pairs and pairing offsets.

Portrait and landscape are both supported. Pages fit as one centered, contiguous pair, with a common height and their original proportions; they do not sit in separate halves of the display. Continuous pairs use the available width.

An offset of 0 gives 1–2, 3–4; an offset of 1 gives 1, 2–3, 4–5. The first n pages remain single. Offsets are chapter overrides unless “Use for this manga” is selected. Reset chapter returns to the manga default.

Panoramas, long strips and an odd final page remain single. Original image splitting is suspended while pairs are active; its preferences remain intact. Guided reading and paired reading are alternative layouts with independent saved settings.

Pairing does not concatenate bitmaps or renumber pages. Shared links, bookmarks, tracking, room positions and drawing refer to original pages. Loading and retry are independent per page; a missing partner cannot complete a chapter.

Settings are stored separately from image caches using opaque source/title/chapter references. Complete .nyabk backups include this optional document; older backups contain no paired-reading settings. No extension protocol changes are required.
